Project Details

Skyrim level design prototype

The Signal Below

A Skyrim Special Edition project with a branching dungeon layout and scripted interaction concepts. The project includes a level-flow design, a plugin cell, and Papyrus controller source.

In development

Design Goals

Teach the player the dungeon's spatial language, give them a meaningful route choice, then test that understanding under pressure. The story is centered on a buried Dwemer relay waking beneath a ruined watchpost, and the player restores three signal stones while hostile scavengers close in.

Role
Level Designer / Technical Designer
Tools

Plugin

Cell
TSBSignalBelow01 - The Signal Below

Level Flow Concept

A. Ruined Entry B. First Contact Camp C. Split Chamber D1. Aqueduct Ledge D2. Crypt Floor E. Signal Puzzle F. Shortcut Descent G. Bridge Holdout H. Exit Vista

Readability

Landmarks Pull The Player Forward

Use warm light to mark forward progress, cool light for optional spaces, and the relay silhouette as a landmark before the puzzle chamber.

Route Choice

High Ledge Or Low Crypt

Offer sightlines and exposure on the high route, with cover and a tighter approach on the low route. Reconnect both paths before the signal chamber.

Scripted Interaction

Three Signals, One Gate

The puzzle controller source tracks unique activators, advances quest stages, plays feedback, and opens a gate after three signals.

Combat Climax

Bridge Holdout Finale

The holdout controller source manages enemy waves, checks for living enemies, and unlocks an exit after the encounter.
